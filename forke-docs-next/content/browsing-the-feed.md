The feed lists tasks you are eligible to claim.

## Filters

| Filter | Use it to |
| --- | --- |
| Tech stack | Find tasks in React, Python, Go and more |
| Level | See work at or below your current level |
| Reward | Set a minimum or maximum bounty |
| Estimated time | Match a task to the time you have, from 30 minutes to 4 hours |

## Reading a task card

Each card shows the title, task type (for example bug fix), stack, estimated time, bounty and the level and trust requirements.

## Why some tasks are hidden

Tasks are gated by [level](#levels-and-unlocks), [skill track](#skill-tracks) and [trust score](#trust-score). Keep shipping to unlock more.
