These terms appear throughout Forke and these docs.

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Bounty | A scoped task with a fixed reward, sized between 30 minutes and 4 hours |
| Claim | Reserving a bounty so nobody else can take it |
| Forke branch | An isolated branch Forke creates for your work. You never push to the owner's main branch |
| Escrow | The reward is deposited before the task goes live and released when the PR merges |
| Verdict card | A plain-English summary of a submission for the task owner |
| XP and level | Points earned from approved tasks that raise your level from 1 to 25 |
| Skill track | Frontend, Backend, Fullstack or DevOps. Tracks gate specialised bounties |
| Trust score | Your reliability record, which gates higher-value work |

## How the pieces fit

A bounty is gated by your **level**, **skill track** and **trust score**. You claim it, work in a **Forke branch**, and submit a PR. The **review pipeline** produces a **verdict card**. When the owner approves, **escrow** pays you and you earn **XP**.
