Every approved task is added to your public profile automatically. It is timestamped, rated and linked to the merged PR, so it works as a resume that compiles.

## What your profile shows

- Completed task history linked to merged PRs
- An activity graph of your shipping streaks
- Your level and earned badges
- Your verified developer identity

## Public profile URL

Your public page is available at `forke.space/<username>`. The public URL unlocks at level 3. See [Levels and unlocks](#levels-and-unlocks).

## Share it

Link your profile on your CV, LinkedIn or GitHub README. Recruiters read the work, not claims about the work.

> **Note** Only approved, merged work appears. Forfeited or rejected submissions do not.
