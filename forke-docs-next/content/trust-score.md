Your trust score is your reliability record on Forke. It works with your level and skill track to decide which bounties you can claim.

## What goes into it

- **Deadline adherence.** Finishing claimed tasks on time raises it.
- **Review pass rates.** Clean submissions raise it.
- **Dispute history.** Disputes lower it, especially ones you lose.
- **Completion rate.** Forfeiting claimed tasks lowers it over time.

## Why it matters

Larger bounties ask for a higher trust score. A task might require, for example, level 8+ and trust 80%+.

## Raising your score

1. Claim fewer tasks and finish them.
2. Submit focused, in-scope PRs.
3. Respond quickly when an owner requests changes.

> **Note** One forfeit will not end your progress. A pattern of them will slow it. See [Claiming a task](#claiming-a-task).
