Forke is a micro-task marketplace for developers. Founders post small, well-scoped tasks with a bounty. Developers claim them, ship a pull request, and get paid to their UPI the moment the work merges. There are no resumes, interviews or invoices.

## From sign-up to first payout

1. **Sign up** at [forke.space](https://www.forke.space/signin) and connect your GitHub account. See [Connecting GitHub and permissions](#github-connection).
2. **Add your UPI ID** in [Account settings](#account-settings) so payouts can reach you.
3. **Browse the feed** and pick an entry-level task. See [Browsing the bounty feed](#browsing-the-feed).
4. **Claim it.** You get a 20-minute window to activate. See [Claiming a task](#claiming-a-task).
5. **Ship a pull request** from your Forke branch. See [Submitting a pull request](#submitting-a-pr).
6. **Pass review.** Your PR goes through the [4-layer review pipeline](#review-pipeline).
7. **Get paid.** When the owner approves and the PR merges, escrow releases to your UPI.

> **Note** Anyone can claim entry-level tasks on day one. Larger bounties unlock as you level up.
