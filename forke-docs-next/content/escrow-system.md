Escrow is why both sides can trust a stranger on the internet.

## Before the task goes live

The founder deposits the full bounty through Razorpay. The funds are locked, so the money exists before any developer starts.

## During the task

The funds stay locked while the task is claimed, built and reviewed.

## On approval

When the owner approves the [verdict card](#verdict-card) and the PR merges, escrow releases to the developer's UPI, usually within a minute.

## Fees

| Who | What they pay or receive |
| --- | --- |
| Developer | Keeps 100% of the listed bounty |
| Founder | Pays a 10% platform fee on top |

A ₹500 task costs the founder ₹550. The developer receives ₹500.
