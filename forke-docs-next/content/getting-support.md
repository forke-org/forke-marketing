## Start here

Press `Ctrl K` to search these docs, or select **Ask AI** at the bottom right to ask a question.

## Open a support ticket

Use the [support page](https://www.forke.space/support) and include your username and the task or submission number.

## Appeal a dispute

If you disagree with a dispute outcome, contact support with the task number and your reasoning. See [Refunds, timeouts and disputes](#refunds-and-disputes).

## Reach the team

For anything else, use the [contact page](https://www.forke.space/contact).
