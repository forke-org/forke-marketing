These rules keep Forke fair for developers and founders. Breaking them can lower your trust score or lead to account action.

## Rules for everyone

- **No plagiarized code.** Submit work you wrote or are licensed to use.
- **No ghosting.** If you cannot finish a claimed task, forfeit it from your dashboard instead of going silent.
- **No circumventing escrow.** Do not take payment or work outside Forke for tasks posted on Forke.
- **No alt accounts.** One person, one account.
- **Be respectful.** Treat every contributor and founder professionally.

## What happens if rules are broken

| Behaviour | Possible outcome |
| --- | --- |
| Repeated ghosting | Lower trust score, fewer bounties |
| Plagiarised submission | Submission rejected, account review |
| Escrow circumvention or alt accounts | Account suspension |

If you think you were treated unfairly, see [Refunds, timeouts and disputes](#refunds-and-disputes) or [contact support](#getting-support).
