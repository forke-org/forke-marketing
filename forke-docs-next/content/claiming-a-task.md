## Soft reservation

When you claim a task, it is reserved for you and nobody else can take it. This is called a soft reservation.

## The 20-minute timer

You have **20 minutes to activate** the task. If you do not, the reservation ends and the task returns to the queue.

{{claimtimer}}

## What happens when you activate

Forke creates your dedicated working branch. From here you can start building. See [Forke branch workflow](#branch-workflow).

## Forfeiting

You can forfeit a claimed task from your dashboard. It returns to the standby queue for someone else.

> **Important** Forfeits lower your completion rate, which feeds your [trust score](#trust-score). Claim only what you can finish.
