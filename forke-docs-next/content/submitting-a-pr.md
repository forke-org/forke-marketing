## Link your PR

Open a pull request from your Forke branch and link it to the claimed task on Forke.

## Describe your change

Add a short summary of what you changed and how to verify it. The AI review and the owner's verdict use it.

## Pre-submission validation

Before the pipeline runs, Forke validates that your PR is linked to the right task and branch. Fix any validation errors and submit again.

## What happens next

Submitting triggers the [4-layer review pipeline](#review-pipeline). You can watch the status of each layer on your dashboard.

> **Note** Failing a layer does not end your claim. Push a fix and the checks run again.
