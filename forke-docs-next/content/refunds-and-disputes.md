## Timeouts

If a claimed task is not activated or finished in time, the reservation ends and the task returns to the queue. Funds remain in escrow for the next developer.

## Refunds

If a task expires with no accepted work, the founder can request a refund of the escrowed bounty.

## Disputes

If an owner and a developer disagree about a submission, either side can open a dispute. Disputes must be raised within the **48-hour dispute window**.

| Step | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Open a dispute within 48 hours |
| 2 | Forke reviews the submission and the task spec |
| 3 | Funds are released or refunded based on the outcome |

> **Important** Dispute history affects your [trust score](#trust-score).
