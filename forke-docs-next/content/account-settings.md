## Profile details

Update your display name, username and the stacks you work in. Your stacks help the feed show tasks you can finish.

## Notification preferences

Choose which updates you receive, such as claim expiry reminders, review results and payout confirmations.

## UPI payouts

Add the UPI ID where you want to be paid. When a PR merges, escrow releases to this UPI ID, usually within a minute.

> **Important** Double-check your UPI ID before your first payout.
