These prompts are built into the Forke Mail MCP server. In Claude and other apps that support MCP prompts, they appear in the prompt or slash-command menu. Anywhere else, click a card to copy it and paste it into the chat.

::prompts

## Writing your own

The prompts that work best:

- **Name the tools** when you know them: "use list_deliveries with that address".
- **Ask to see the plan first** for anything with several steps: "show me the plan before you create the workflow".
- **Say when to stop**: "only call send_broadcast after I say yes".
- **Use a test key** while building: "create a test API key".

See every tool in the [tools reference](/docs-demo/agents/tools).
