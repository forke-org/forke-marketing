## Projects

A project holds everything for one product: keys, domains, contacts, templates, emails, webhooks and analytics. Projects don't share anything, so a test project can never email your real customers.

- **Create** projects from the project picker at the top of the sidebar.
- **Switch** between them from the same picker. API keys always belong to one project; AI apps work in the project you picked when you connected them (ask them to "switch to my other project").
- **Rename, reset or delete** a project under **Settings**. Resetting clears contacts, emails and templates but keeps keys, domains, webhooks, the team and suppressions.

## Team roles

| Role | Can |
| --- | --- |
| **Owner** | Everything: API keys, team members and roles, moving credits, renaming, resetting or deleting the project. |
| **Admin** | Day-to-day work: templates, contacts, segments, broadcasts, workflows, checking domains, deliveries and analytics. Can't add or delete domains, manage webhooks, remove suppressions, or manage keys and the team. |

Invite people under **Settings → Team**. They join with a link and land in this project.

> [!NOTE]
> AI apps act with your role. If you're an admin, an app you connect can't create API keys either.
