Forke is gated on both sides: founders are approved by a person, and developers earn access by shipping. A few rules keep it that way.

## For everyone

- **Keep it professional.** Harassment or abuse leads to account action.

## For developers

- **Ship your own work.** Plagiarised submissions, or AI output pasted in without understanding it, are removed.
- **Don't claim what you can't start.** Abandoned tasks slow everyone down.
- **Stay in scope.** Don't try to work around the file-scope check.
- **Never commit secrets** and never try to reach the founder's systems beyond the task.

## For founders

- **Scope tasks honestly.** Clear acceptance criteria and a tight file scope are what developers and the review pipeline work against. See [Writing a great task](/docs-demo/forke/posting-tasks).
