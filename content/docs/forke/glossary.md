---
title: Glossary
description: Every term you'll see on Forke, from acceptance criteria to XP, in one place.
---

**Acceptance criteria**: The checkable outcomes a task must meet. The AI review scores the work against them and the founder approves against them. See [Writing a great task](/docs-demo/forke/posting-tasks).

**Activation**: Starting work on a claimed task: creating your branch, pushing a first commit, opening a draft pull request or pressing **I'm Working**. It locks the task to you and starts the deadline.

**Claim**: Reserving a task for 20 minutes. Not ownership until you activate. See [Claiming a task](/docs-demo/forke/claiming).

**Criticality**: How much a task matters if it goes wrong: Personal, Startup or Production. Production work on sensitive areas (auth, payments) is reviewed more strictly.

**Escrow**: The task budget, paid by the founder up front and held by Forke until the work is approved. See [Escrow & payments](/docs-demo/forke/escrow).

**File scope**: The files and folders a task may change. Pull requests that touch anything else are rejected automatically.

**FORKE_SUBMISSION.md**: The short write-up you fill in before opening a pull request: what you changed, why, and how you tested it.

**Level**: Your experience on Forke, from 1 to 25, earned with XP. One of the three access gates. See [Levels & unlocks](/docs-demo/forke/levels).

**Mirror**: Forke's copy of a founder's repository, where all task branches live. Developers never push to the founder's repository.

**Proof of work**: Approved tasks on your public profile, each linked to the merged pull request on GitHub.

**Risk score**: A 0–100 score from the review pipeline that decides who reviews a submission next.

**Skill tier**: Your depth in one track (Frontend, Backend, Fullstack, Mobile, AI/ML). One of the three access gates. See [Skill tracks](/docs-demo/forke/skill-tracks).

**Soft reservation**: The 20 minutes after a claim, when the task is held for you but can still return to the feed.

**Task** (or bounty): A scoped unit of work, 30 minutes to about 4 hours, with a fixed budget, a time estimate, skill tags, an XP reward and a file scope.

**Task branch**: `task/<id>-<slug>`, the branch in the mirror that your pull request targets.

**Trust score**: How reliable you are, computed from your history. One of the three access gates. See [Trust score](/docs-demo/forke/trust-score).

**Verdict card**: The founder's review screen: a plain-English summary of a submission with scores, flags and a recommendation. See [Reading the verdict card](/docs-demo/forke/verdict-card).

**XP**: Experience points. Earned from approved tasks, speed and ratings; they raise your level. See [Earning XP](/docs-demo/forke/xp).
