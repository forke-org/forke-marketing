Escrow removes the oldest fear on both sides of freelance work. The developer worries "will I get paid?", and the founder worries "will I get work worth paying for?". Forke holds the money in the middle, so neither side can let the other down.

::demo{name="payout"}

## How money moves

:::steps

### The founder funds escrow

When posting, the founder pays the budget plus Forke's 10% through Razorpay. Forke holds the budget in escrow before anyone can claim the task, so developers can see the money is there.

### The work is reviewed

If the submission fails the automated checks, nothing moves. If the founder asks for changes, the money stays held while the developer revises.

### The founder approves

Forke merges a clean pull request into the founder's repository and releases the full budget to the developer's UPI straight away. There's no withdrawal step and no waiting period.

:::

## What Forke charges

A flat 10%, paid by the founder on top of the budget. The developer always receives the full budget.

| Budget | Founder pays | Developer receives | Forke keeps |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ₹500 | ₹550 | ₹500 | ₹50 |
| ₹800 | ₹880 | ₹800 | ₹80 |
| ₹2,500 | ₹2,750 | ₹2,500 | ₹250 |
