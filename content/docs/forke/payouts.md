## Your UPI ID

Add your UPI ID in [Settings](https://dashboard.forke.space/settings) before your first submission. When a founder approves your work, the full task budget is sent there straight away.

- **No withdrawal step.** There's no wallet to cash out and no waiting period.
- **You get the whole budget.** Forke's 10% is paid by the founder on top.
- **Higher levels unlock bigger payout tiers** (intermediate tiers from level 7).

> [!WARNING]
> Double-check the UPI ID. Payouts are instant, so money sent to a mistyped ID can't be pulled back by Forke.

Track every payout in [Earnings](https://dashboard.forke.space/earnings) and money that's still held in [Escrow](https://dashboard.forke.space/escrow).

## Notifications

Choose what you hear about in Settings:

- Claim reminders, so a reservation doesn't run out
- Review verdicts and change requests
- Payout confirmations
- Level-ups and achievements

The bell in the app ([Notifications](https://dashboard.forke.space/notifications)) shows the same updates.
