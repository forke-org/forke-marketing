title: Trust score

---

Your trust score measures how reliable you are to work with, not how well you code. It's computed from your real history and can't be self-reported.

## What moves it

| Factor | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Finishing the tasks you start | Strong positive |
| Hitting deadlines | Positive |
| Founder ratings | Positive |
| Submitting early | Positive |
| Consistent AI review scores | Positive |
| Repeated revision requests | Negative |
| Abandoning a task after activating it | Strong negative |

## Why it matters

- **It's one of the three access gates.** Sensitive tasks (production, payments) ask for high trust.
- **It unlocks reviewing.** At level 15 with a trust score of 90% or more, you can become a reviewer.
- **It speeds up disputes.** Higher-level, trusted developers get faster dispute resolution.

## How to keep it high

1. Only claim what you can start now and finish inside the estimate.
2. Read the acceptance criteria before you claim, so you don't need revisions.
3. If something blocks you, tell Forke early through [support](/docs-demo/forke/support) instead of letting the deadline pass.
