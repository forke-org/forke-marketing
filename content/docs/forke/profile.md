Forke profiles have no résumé and no self-written skills list. They show only work that was shipped, reviewed and paid for.

## What's on your profile

- **Every approved task**, added automatically, with the date, the tech used, the XP earned and the founder's rating.
- **A link to the merged pull request** on GitHub for each one. You and Forke both appear as contributors on the founder's repository, so anyone can check.
- **Your level, tier badges and achievements.**
- **An activity graph** of what you shipped and when.

You never add or edit an entry by hand. If it's on your profile, it happened.

## Your own address

At **level 3** you unlock a custom profile address: `forke.space/<your username>`. Put it on your CV, your GitHub README or your LinkedIn: it's a portfolio that can't be faked.

## Achievements

Badges you collect along the way, shown on your profile: First Blood, Bug Hunter, Clutch Commit, Untouchable, Monster Sprint, Streak God, Loot Goblin, Boss Mode and Forke Legend.
